# CricketZone Complete Product Demo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 5-8 minutes
**Style:** Premium, fast-paced, smooth transitions, highlight cursor.
**Setup before recording:** 
1. Login to your local app.
2. Go to `http://localhost:3000/api/seed-demo` in a new tab. This will instantly generate the Eagles Cricket Club, Shangla Super League, teams, players, and match.
3. Switch your current organization from the top-left dropdown to **Eagles Cricket Club**.
4. You are now ready to record!

---

### Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
- **Visuals:** Start on the Dashboard Overview (showing the new stats). Move cursor smoothly.
- **Caption/Voiceover:** "Welcome to CricketZone — the most advanced digital ecosystem for cricket organizations. From local clubs to professional leagues, manage everything in one place."
- **Action:** Highlight the beautiful dark mode UI, stats cards, and activity chart.

### Scene 2: Tournament Management (0:15 - 0:45)
- **Action:** Click on "Tournaments" in the sidebar. Click on "Shangla Super League 2026".
- **Visuals:** Show the tournament dashboard. Click through the "Teams", "Matches", and "Standings" tabs quickly to show the rich interface.
- **Caption:** "Create professional tournaments in seconds. Configure formats, rules, and track points tables automatically."

### Scene 3: Teams & Players (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Action:** Click on "Teams" in the sidebar. Open "Kaardan Warriors". 
- **Visuals:** Show the team's professional card. Switch to the "Roster" tab to show the players assigned.
- **Action:** Click on "Players" in the sidebar. Scroll through the list of professional players. 
- **Caption:** "Manage franchise teams and deep player profiles with detailed statistics."

### Scene 4: Match Preparation & Toss (1:30 - 2:30)
- **Action:** Click on "Match Center" -> "Kaardan Warriors vs Abseen Heroes".
- **Visuals:** The Match Workspace. Click "Perform Toss".
- **Action:** Select Toss Winner (e.g., Kaardan Warriors) and Decision (Bat). 
- **Action:** Add Playing XI for both teams. Select Captain and Wicket Keeper.
- **Caption:** "Complete pre-match workflows: Digital Toss, Playing XI selection, and squad management."

### Scene 5: Live Scoring (2:30 - 4:00)
- **Action:** Start the match. Open the Scoring Terminal.
- **Visuals:** The modern scoring dashboard.
- **Action:** 
  1. Score a dot ball.
  2. Score a boundary (4 runs).
  3. Score a Six.
  4. Click "Wicket", select dismissal type, new batter, and cross-over status.
- **Caption:** "Industry-leading live scoring interface. Track every ball, partnership, and wagon wheel in real-time."

### Scene 6: Public Fan Zone (4:00 - 5:00)
- **Action:** Open a new Incognito window (or use a different browser profile). Navigate to `/fanzone/eagles-XYZ` (Check the exact slug from your dashboard).
- **Visuals:** The stunning public Fan Zone. 
- **Action:** Click on "Live". Show the hero match card. Click into the Match Center.
- **Visuals:** The public scorecard updating live.
- **Caption:** "Give your fans a premium viewing experience. No login required. Live ball-by-ball commentary and real-time scorecards."

### Scene 7: Outro (5:00 - 5:15)
- **Visuals:** Fast montage (1 second each) of Dashboard -> Tournament -> Team -> Match -> FanZone.
- **Caption/Text on screen:** "CricketZone — From Local Cricket to Professional Digital Management."
- **End screen:** Logo fading out.

---
**Tips for Recording:**
- Don't rush mouse movements. Use smooth, deliberate clicks.
- If you make a mistake, pause, take a breath, and repeat the action. You can cut the mistake out in editing.
- Ensure your browser is in full screen mode (F11) to hide tabs and bookmarks.
